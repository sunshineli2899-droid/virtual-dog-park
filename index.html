Dog Encyclopedia

Three breeds. So much personality.

01 /The curious explorer

Beagle

A big nose for adventure.

Size
Small to medium, with a sturdy build.
Personality
Curious, cheerful, and led by their nose.
Everyday care
Enjoy daily walks and sniffing games. Brush weekly and check their floppy ears.

02 /The sunshine friend

Golden Retriever

A heart as golden as their coat.

Size
Large, with a strong, active body.
Personality
Friendly, playful, and eager to learn.
Everyday care
Make time for daily walks and play. Brush several times a week to manage shedding.

03 /The clever companion

Poodle

Curly coat. Bright little mind.

Size
Comes in toy, miniature, and standard sizes.
Personality
Smart, active, and quick to learn.
Everyday care
Offer daily exercise and learning games. Brush often and arrange regular haircuts.

♡

A little love goes a long way. Every dog needs fresh water, good food, gentle training, and regular vet visits.
